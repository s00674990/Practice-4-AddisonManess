Piet Mondrian

"The first aim in a painting should be universal expression." Mondrian believed in creating a synthesis between art and life. Even though he did not begin his art career amking abstract work, after seeing an exhibition of "Cubist Art" in Amsterdam in 1911, it peaked his interest in creating abstract pieces.

Mondrian didn't just want to create abstract pieces though, he wanted to feel freedom as an artist. Mondrian dedicated the next years of his life developing this ability in Holland.

Born in 1872 in Amersfoort, Utrecht

Studied painting at Amsterdam Academy from 1892-1894

Joined the Theosophic Organization in 1909

Joined van Doesburg in the de Stijl movement in 1917-1925

Passed away in 1944

Less, until it holds

Mondrian engaged with abstraction in a philosophical way, which led to the founding of De Stijl.

De Stijl was a collective of architects, artists, and the like who were working to bridge the gap between art and life through their work.
